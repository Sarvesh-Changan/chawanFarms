## PRD Appendix B — starting conflicts

These are retained from Appendix B and expanded with evidence and implementation treatment.

1. **Cancellation conflict — unresolved.** Slide 28 says: “No Refund, Cancellation or Postponement shall be accepted for whatsoever Reasons given by the guest once confirmed & paid.” The same slide later says: “25% charged when booking cancelled seven days before checking.” Do not calculate refunds or publish one interpretation until D-2 is decided. Keep both source statements in a draft/versioned policy record.
2. **Payment timing — unresolved.** Slide 28 says booking is confirmed against 100% payment and a cheque is confirmed after realisation. It does not say whether partial advances are accepted. See D-1 and D-36.
3. **Minimum group of 10 — scope unresolved.** The minimum appears on the Part B slide only. It must not silently be applied to Part A, Part C, or the picnic package. See D-4.
4. **Part C meal inclusion — ambiguous.** Slide 26 says the included meals are breakfast, evening tea/nasta and dinner, while the menu heading says “Lunch / Dinner Menu”. Lunch is not listed in the inclusion sentence. See D-23.
5. **Picnic food/pricing — ambiguous.** Slide 28 says adults ₹1100 and kids ₹750, including breakfast/tea, lunch and evening tea, followed by “Veg / Non-Veg Meal (chicken)”. It does not state whether the same price applies to both options, or whether chicken is the only non-vegetarian option. See D-22.
6. **Extras have no amounts.** Mutton/chicken are charged per kg; fish/shellfish and barbecue are extra; bullock cart is extra. No price, unit weight, serving size or calculation rule is supplied. Store prices as null and show “Contact us” until D-18 is answered.
7. **Rafting, jungle safari, Jain food and cottage.** The old website contains jungle safari/night safari, Jain packages, cottage stay and other activities. The PDF contains white-water river rafting and jungle trail, but not “jungle safari”, Jain food or cottage accommodation. Keep website-only items as DRAFT/unpublished under D-6; confirm rafting operator and liability under D-25.
8. **“Safety Major”.** Slide 29 visibly labels the safety section “Safety Major”. This is likely a typo for “Safety Measure” or “Safety Measures”, but the public heading must not be silently changed. See D-21.
9. **Low-resolution imagery.** Slides are collages and do not provide production-ready originals or rights/consent records for identifiable people. Do not upscale or publish them as the final gallery. See D-14 and D-32.

## Document/schema/security conflicts and gaps

| Public booking versus authentication | PRD/BUILD_GUIDE describe a public booking flow and an anonymous confirmation prompt, while SECURITY requires verified email before the first booking-linked action. | Decide whether anonymous users may submit a booking request, or whether only verified customers may create bookings. Keep anonymous quick enquiries separate if required (D-28). |
| Mutation pipeline | ARCHITECTURE’s generic pipeline says authenticate/authorise on every mutation; public forms necessarily have no logged-in user. | Define an explicit public-action branch: rate limit → validate → bot check → service, with no customer permission check, while protected actions retain authz. |
